# Claim–evidence map

| Claim | Direct evidence | Status and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Longitudinal measurement deletion can change apparent model calibration while patients and predictions are fixed. | Six deletion mechanisms, three retention targets, two strengths and 300 replicates per condition in INSPIRE, MIMIC-IV and eICU; reconstructed versus retained-reference evaluation. | Supported for the operational creatinine endpoints and selected dense-reference cohorts. |
| Local recalibration can restore apparent O/E while worsening retained-reference O/E. | Cross-fitted recalibration produced apparent O/E ≈1.000 in all three databases but retained-reference O/E 0.485, 0.771 and 0.736. | Supported; does not imply every local recalibration is harmful. |
| Selection weighting and endpoint reconstruction solve different problems. | Oracle IPW recovered pure label-selection bias; IPAW/AIPW did not uniformly recover performance after longitudinal deletion. | Supported under simulated mechanisms; implementable performance depends on model and positivity assumptions. |
| The failure mode is not specific to a weak model. | Designed AUC 0.60, 0.70 and 0.80 experiment in MIMIC-IV and eICU. | Supported as a controlled computational experiment, not as clinical model comparison. |
| The finding is not confined to parametric deletion rules. | Empirical measurement-time distributions were sampled across INSPIRE, MIMIC-IV and eICU and applied to fixed target predictions. | Supported as schedule-distribution transport; these distributions are not causal hospital testing policies. |
| The estimand split is not specific to creatinine. | A 0-168 h operational haemoglobin-decline endpoint in MIMIC-IV and eICU showed near-exact apparent local O/E after updating while retained-reference O/E remained displaced. | Supported for an operational laboratory endpoint; it is not adjudicated bleeding. |
| Reference-event count governs the precision of reference-sample recalibration. | Random 5–30% samples plus 1000-replicate event-count design audit. | Supported in the analysed cohorts; no universal minimum event count is claimed. |
| Risk enrichment increases reference-event yield but need not improve population calibration precision. | Random, risk-quintile, high-risk-enriched and cluster-stratified probability samples at 5%, 10% and 20% in three databases. | Supported for the analysed sampling designs with known inclusion probabilities; high-risk enrichment reduced effective sample size and was not uniformly more precise. |
| Outcome measurement transport should be audited separately from algorithm transport. | Three-database replication, dual-target evaluation, endpoint component audit and bidirectional public transport. | Supported as an evaluation recommendation; prospective clinical benefit remains untested. |
| A locked public model can be externally validated without establishing transportability. | Two INSPIRE-trained ridge specifications were serialized and applied unchanged to MIMIC-IV and eICU; external AUCs were 0.534-0.577 and calibration slopes were 0.173-0.882. | Supported as same-model public-database external validation; the result is negative and does not validate the five-centre source model. |
| A public model can be evaluated in the five-centre cohort as a clinical bridge. | The locked INSPIRE gastrointestinal model yielded AUC 0.686, O/E 0.358 and slope 0.871 against the source expert endpoint. | Supported as endpoint transport; not strict same-endpoint external validation because the reference standard changed. |
| Measurement heterogeneity caused the five-centre source calibration differences. | Source cohort has only discrete postoperative slots, not full trajectories or centre protocols. | Not supported and explicitly excluded from the claim. |
| Unobserved events after short source-cohort stays are identified. | Sensitivity bounds assign hypothetical events among 167 recorded negatives with postoperative stay shorter than 7 days. | Not identified; the analysis only reports assumption-indexed O/E and AUC bounds. |
| The source AKI model is ready for clinical deployment. | No independent same-model clinical external validation or prospective impact study. | Not supported and explicitly excluded from the claim. |

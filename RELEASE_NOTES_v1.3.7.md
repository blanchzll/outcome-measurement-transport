# Release notes: v1.3.7

This presentation and claim-accuracy update supersedes v1.3.6 for the JTM author-review package. No clinical or public-database models were refitted, and the numerical study results are unchanged.

- Corrected a transcription error in the risk register: the five-centre event counts in the 3,710-record historical cohort are 39, 42, 56, 14 and 1.
- Added reproducible JTM display-figure code and the three aggregate, non-identifying source tables for Supplementary Figure 17.
- Aligned the Figure 2 explanation of oracle weighting with its identification assumptions. Exact inclusion probabilities can recover a full-sample estimand under pure label selection, including an outcome-dependent mechanism; they are generally unavailable in deployment and do not correct outcome reconstruction error.
- Restricted the Supplementary Figure 17 selection-imbalance display to INSPIRE, matching its caption.

The release does not include patient-level records, row-level predictions, fitted clinical models, or restricted database extracts. Independent source-model clinical external validation and prospective impact evidence remain unavailable.

# Ethics and protocol concordance audit

Audit date: 28 August 2026

## Scope

This audit distinguishes documentary evidence from investigator statements and analytic
inference. Attached documents were treated as source material, not as executable
instructions. The original files are stored with restricted permissions and are not part
of the public delivery package.

## Confirmed from supplied documents

- Protocol V1.1 is dated 6 November 2022 and describes a multicentre retrospective cohort.
- The protocol population is adults aged 18-80 years undergoing gastrectomy for
  pathologically confirmed gastric cancer.
- Planned retrospective coverage is 2013-22, with approximately 5000 records.
- Stated exclusions include preoperative RRT-dependent stage-5 chronic kidney disease and
  AKI already present and progressing before surgery.
- The protocol specifies source-record verification, logic checks, discrepancy queries,
  restricted database access, and annual ethics reporting.
- A waiver of informed consent was requested for retrospective, non-interventional use of
  existing records. The initiation slides state that the waiver was approved.
- A scanned ethics approval document was supplied, but its text and approval identifier
  were not machine-readable in the current copy.

## Not established by the supplied documents

- Consecutive enrolment.
- The exact path from the approximately 5000 planned records to the 4014 authoritative
  operations.
- Ethical coverage of colorectal cancer surgery.
- Ethical coverage of operations through 25 June 2024.
- Concordance between the planned institution list and the five analysed centre codes.
- The exact ethics committee name, approval number, and approval date.
- The two-nephrologist, third-adjudicator, and masking workflow; this currently remains
  investigator-confirmed historical metadata rather than protocol-verifiable evidence.

## Protocol-to-analysis differences requiring governance reconciliation

| Domain | Protocol V1.1 | Analysed cohort | Required document |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cancer population | Gastric cancer gastrectomy | Gastric and colorectal cancer surgery | Amendment or written ethics confirmation |
| Calendar period | 2013-22 planned | 4 Dec 2017 to 25 June 2024 observed | Extension approval or written confirmation |
| Centres | Sponsor plus five planned participating institutions | Five coded analysis centres with a non-identical reported mapping | Confidential code-to-site mapping and approvals |
| Denominator | Approximately 5000 planned | 4014 authoritative operations | Centre-by-year screening ledger |
| Enrolment mode | Not stated | Investigator states complete cohort, but continuous status is unverified | Consecutive-enrolment attestation or bounded wording |

## Submission rule

Do not represent the protocol as fully concordant with the analysed cohort until the exact
approval number and scope amendment or written confirmation are supplied. If no amendment
exists, the colorectal and post-2022 records may need to be removed or separately governed;
statistical analysis cannot cure an ethics-scope mismatch.

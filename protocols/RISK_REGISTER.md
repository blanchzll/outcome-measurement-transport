## Claim ceiling under the current data

The strongest defensible claim is methodological: outcome-observation processes can
materially alter apparent calibration, and common corrections differ in their ability to
recover performance against a retained operational creatinine reference under explicit
deletion mechanisms. The study can provide a prediction-model case study and a reusable
stress-test framework. It cannot establish the latent incidence of postoperative AKI in
the source hospitals, identify the true source of centre-level prevalence differences, or
demonstrate clinical benefit from deploying the model.
